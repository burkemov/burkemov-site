Frequently asked questions

What's the turnaround time?

24 hours on average for short-form video — ads, Reels, and clips. Larger projects, like brand films, get a timeline agreed on in the proposal.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the format, length, and volume. Message me on WhatsApp with what you need and I'll send a quick quote, no obligation.

How do I send the raw footage?

Google Drive, WeTransfer, or whatever tool you prefer. I'll take it from there and organize everything.

What if I need revisions?

Revisions are part of the process: you review the preview and I refine it until it's right, within the agreed scope.

Do you take monthly retainers?

Yes. For podcasts, social media, and ads with recurring volume, a monthly package usually works out better.
